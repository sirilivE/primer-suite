About

Primer Suite runs identically as a web page and as an offline desktop application (Electron). All computation happens locally; no sequence ever leaves your computer.

Full instructions: User guide · Source: github.com/sirilivE/primer-suite

Methods

  • Tm: nearest-neighbour model with SantaLucia (1998) unified parameters; salt correction 0.368·(N−1)·ln[Na⁺]; Mg²⁺ converted to Na⁺-equivalent as 120·√([Mg²⁺]−[dNTP]) (von Ahsen 2001). Primer concentration enters as C/4.
  • Dimers: every antiparallel ungapped alignment of two primers is scored; stems (≥2 bp) are summed with nearest-neighbour ΔG, duplex initiation and internal-loop penalties (SantaLucia & Hicks 2004). The most stable structure and the most stable structure involving a 3′-terminal base are reported.
  • Hairpins: all stem/loop combinations with loop ≥ 3 nt, scored with stacking + hairpin-loop penalties.
  • Binding check: exhaustive scan of both strands allowing up to N mismatches with IUPAC support; Tm of mismatched sites is estimated from the matched stacks only.
  • Design: Primer3-style candidate enumeration and penalty scoring (Tm, length, GC, clamp, self-structure, 3′ stability, uniqueness) followed by pair scoring (ΔTm, cross-dimer, product size).
  • Multiplex: full pairwise cross-dimer matrix, 3′-end dimer detection, per-primer interaction counts and a greedy partition into compatible pools.
  • Specificity: primers are scanned against the selected organism's background sequences (≤ 2 mismatches, last 3 bases of the 3′ end matched); every forward/reverse site combination within 4 kb is reported as a potential product.

Rules of thumb: cross/self-dimer ΔG below −6 kcal/mol deserves attention, below −9 is usually a problem; a 3′-terminal stem below −5 (or a 3′-anchored structure below −6) can produce primer-dimer product. Hairpins below −3 (or any 3′-end hairpin) are undesirable.
